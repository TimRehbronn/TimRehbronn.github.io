From **January 2020 to June 2021** I was part of the electrical powertrain department of
[Ecurie Aix](https://ecurie-aix.de), the Formula Student team of RWTH Aachen University — a team of around 80 students that
designs and builds an electric race car every season.

- **Jan 2020 – Sep 2020 · Electrical Powertrain:** development of the car's electrical system.
- **Oct 2020 – Jun 2021 · Head of Electrical Powertrain:** led a team of seven students, planned and managed the department's
  projects, and was responsible for the electrical system of the team's self-developed electric race cars
  (during a semester off).

The 2021 car, the [eace09 "Nuri"](https://ecurie-aix.de/car/eace09/), was the team's ninth electric car — an evolution of its
predecessor with a focus on lightweight design, reliability and maintainability to allow a long testing phase.
