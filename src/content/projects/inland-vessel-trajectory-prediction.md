Safe automated navigation on inland waterways needs to anticipate where other vessels will be in the next minutes.
Purely data-driven predictors can produce physically implausible motion; purely kinematic models miss intent and context.

I am working on **learned, physics-inspired models** that combine deep learning with ship kinematics and are trained on
**AIS data** of inland vessels. More details will follow with the publication.
