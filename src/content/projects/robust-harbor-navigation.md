In ports and confined waterways, GNSS reception is often sparse or unreliable, yet automated vessels need accurate and
robust position estimates exactly there. This is the focus of my current work:

- **Sensors:** IMU, LiDAR, radar, camera and GNSS — combining sensors that fail in different ways.
- **Estimation back-ends:** factor graph optimization and an iterated error-state EKF.
- **Setting:** GNSS-sparse, constrained harbor environments with quay walls, moored ships and tight maneuvering space.

**Code, abstract and paper will follow later in 2026.** Target venue: *IFAC Conference on Control Applications in Marine
Systems, Robotics and Vehicles (CAMS) 2027*.
